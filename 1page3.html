Our fictional origin story

One idea.
A whole lot of spark.

It started with a conversation after class: “Why does planning a night out have to be so complicated?” We wanted an experience that brought students, families, and friends together without making the planning feel like another assignment.

Our fictional Columbia Fireflies business grew from that idea. We combined baseball, themed events, and group experiences to create more than a ticket purchase—we wanted to create a reason to connect.

Our mission: bring Columbia together through welcoming, memorable baseball experiences.
